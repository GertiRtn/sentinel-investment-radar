# AGENTS.md — read this before changing anything

Sentinel is an investment **research and discovery** platform. It scans beyond the watchlist, turns market news into dated, evidence-backed research cases, and measures what happened afterwards. Its value is that its own record can be trusted. The invariants below protect that.

**Starting a session:** read this file, `docs/HANDOFF.md` and the latest founder brief, then state back what you understand the current state to be before writing code. When the founder's latest explicit instruction conflicts with an older proposal in these docs, the founder's instruction wins. The invariants are not "older proposals": changing one needs the founder's explicit approval.

Last revised 2026-11-12, when development moved from ChatGPT/Codex to Claude Code. The baseline is the deployed Sites code (snapshot v36), not the September `main`.

---

## Invariants — breaking one is a regression, regardless of what it enables

1. **Nothing is mutated.** No UPDATE or DELETE on captured data, facts, journal entries, resolutions, criteria, evaluations, discovery candidates, cases or outcomes. Change is expressed by appending a superseding record. Enforced by **36 SQL triggers** (`RAISE(ABORT, ...)`) in `drizzle/`. Do not remove or weaken a trigger. If a migration needs one dropped, stop and ask.

2. **`known_from` governs every read.** Any query must be answerable as of a past timestamp using only records where `known_from <= T`. For SEC facts, `known_from` is the **filing date**, never the period end (`e.filed + 'T23:59:59.999Z'` in `lib/sentinel/financial.ts`). Keep publication time, first-observed time and assessment time separate for news and cases.

3. **Unevaluable is not passing.** A check that cannot run returns `unknown`/`unavailable`/`WAIT`. It never returns pass, never renders green, never renders blank. In `evaluate()`, any `unknown` child forces the whole result to `unknown`.

4. **No value reaches the UI without provenance:** source, source timestamp (or explicit null), ingest timestamp, staleness and confidence.

5. **No model-generated numbers.** No probability, forecast, score or estimate produced by a language model reaches the UI. Probabilities are the user's own, recorded before the outcome. Extraction from a source must cite the span; uncited claims are dropped.

6. **No composite scores presented as forecasts.** A queue-priority order is research routing, never a return forecast. *Amended 2026-11-12 with the founder's explicit approval* ("We have to build such algorithms or systems that recommend that score based on the information we have"): one composite is allowed, the **evidence score** (`lib/sentinel/evidence-score.ts`, `evidence-score-v1`). It is a published checklist of pass/fail/unknown checks with fixed points (sum = 100), computed only from stored, sourced records, shown with its full checklist and always labelled as evidence strength, **never** as a probability, expected return or buy signal. Unknown checks score 0 and are shown as unknown; below 60 known points there is no score. Changing a weight or check means a new version, never an edit in place. Its usefulness is judged by outcomes per score band before anyone relies on it.

7. **Alerts inform, never instruct.** No "buy", "sell" or price target in any alert or case body. `INVESTIGATE` means "warrants research", `WAIT` means "required evidence is missing". Buy/sell labels are a future experiment that needs explicit rules and prospective validation first.

8. **Credentials stay server-side.** No key in a client bundle, `localStorage`, a commit, a handoff note or any user-visible field. `.env` stays gitignored; `.env.example` carries empty values. **This repository is public.**

9. **Tier 5 sources are not ingested:** no social media, price commentary or TV.

10. **Outcomes are recorded for everything assessed, including WAIT and rejected cases.** Without that comparison group, outcome tracking proves nothing.

## Current system (deployed v36 plus PRs to #28; `node --test tests/*.test.mjs`: 296/296 on 2026-11-25 on branch `research-eval-stage1`)

- Server-side persistence on Cloudflare D1, raw captures in D1/R2 with SHA-256 dedup.
- SEC: identity, filings, annual + quarterly figures, token bucket below SEC's ceiling, identifying User-Agent.
- Discovery: Alpaca market news (`lib/sentinel/discovery-collector.ts`), up to 5×50 items per run, checkpointed.
- Cases: `lib/sentinel/discovery-case.ts`, up to 6 per run with a 35 s budget, one slot in three oldest-first. Only `topic match` candidates are assessed. Since 2026-11-13 the queue (`discoveryQueue`) is the last 96 hours, read once per run and matched in memory; older reports are not assessed. The direct-role rule is `headline-relevance-v3` (hyphenated names, legal suffixes and exchange tags no longer hide the verb).
- News direction (`lib/sentinel/news-direction.ts`, `news-direction-v1`, 2026-11-15, founder decision): every report is labelled **good / bad / mixed / neutral** from fixed word lists over the headline (the provider summary only when the headline says nothing), with the matched words shown. Scandal and solvency words (fraud, investigation, SEC charges, class action, restatement, short-seller report, recall, going concern, delisting, bankruptcy) always make it bad news; a cancelled or withdrawn deal is bad news even though it names the deal. Stored on new cases; older cases are computed from their stored text. **Bad and mixed news are never shown as ideas** (Where to look first, morning brief takes); they are still assessed, recorded and measured, as the comparison group. Wording only: not a forecast.
- Idea list (`lib/sentinel/idea-select.ts` `idea-select-v2` since 2026-11-15: only good news can meet the rules, neutral wording at most 'closest', bad and mixed never shown but counted; `GET /api/ideas`, `components/sentinel/idea-list.tsx`): "Where to look first" on Brief and Discover. Rule-meeting cases, then the closest ones with what they lack, or an explanation when nothing qualifies. Research routing only (invariant 6).
- Hourly refresh stages (`lib/sentinel/pipeline.ts`, `pipeline-v1`, 2026-11-13): the MCP tool reports a status and elapsed time per stage, takes an optional `stages` list (default all), sets `isError` when any stage failed, and appends a `pipeline_run` record (read it with `GET /api/pipeline`). Filings stop starting new companies after 30 s (protective cap, unmeasured). The assessment batch is still 6 cases / 35 s; do not raise it without stage times from the run records.
- Security identity (`lib/sentinel/security-identity.ts`, `security-identity-v2` since 2026-11-12): active US equity + exact symbol + one SEC ticker + no other-instrument words in the name (ADR/ADS, units, warrants, rights, preferred, funds, trusts, ETFs, notes, named share classes). v1 also required the words "Common Stock", which rejected most NYSE names. Stored cases keep their v1 result.
- Screen: `researchDecision()` in `lib/sentinel/price-assessment.ts` (`research-screen-v1`).
- Indicative P/E from an IEX midpoint and reported annual diluted EPS; cash-flow scenarios (fixed 12% discount, ±5% growth, 5 years).
- Outcomes: `lib/sentinel/outcome-collector.ts`, 5/20/60/120 matched trading days vs SPY, gross, up to 8 per run.
- Journal, thesis memos, kill criteria.
- Practice v2 (`lib/sentinel/practice.ts`, `app/api/practice/route.ts`, `components/sentinel/practice.tsx`): $10,000 virtual; open/close priced by the server from a recorded IEX midpoint (never typed), SPY recorded at every trade as the position's benchmark; the user's own conviction 0–100 required; thesis, exit plan and kill condition optional since 2026-11-14 (founder decision, made on the Site and reconciled 2026-11-15); review prompts at 1 week / 1 month / 3 months; scorecard by evidence band and conviction band (median only from 5 trades); one `practice_mark` per UTC day from the hourly run. Gross returns. The old $50 manual ledger (`paper_trade`) is kept read-and-write under a fold.
- Practice risk check (`lib/sentinel/practice-risk.ts` `practice-risk-v1`, `GET /api/practice-risk`, `components/sentinel/practice-risk.tsx`): on demand and read-only, virtual positions only. Concentration and coverage first; then a seeded historical bootstrap of whole shared trading days (10,000 paths, 20 sessions, 252-day lookback, at least 120 days required) giving VaR/ES at 95%, percentiles, volatility, correlation and illustrative stresses. Insufficient history → unavailable. Daily bars come from the shared archive `lib/sentinel/daily-bars.ts` (source `alpaca:iex:daily-bars:*`, reused 6 h), also used by the case sheet. Stores nothing, never trades, and is not part of the hourly run.
- Discover ranking (`lib/sentinel/discover-rank.ts`, `discover-rank-v2` since 2026-11-13): last 7 days of cases, one row per symbol, highest evidence score first, with a "why here" line; research routing, not a return forecast (invariant 6). v2 lists a probable company mismatch (`name_conflict`) last; v3 (2026-11-15) lists bad or mixed news after the rest, with its label. The raw feed is folded below.
- Event ↔ company match (`lib/sentinel/event-match.ts`, `event-match-v1`, 2026-11-13): whether the stored headline or provider summary names the SEC company. A provider ticker tag alone is `ticker_only`; the ticker's letters used as another name ("ams-OSRAM" tagged AMS) is `name_conflict`. Only `named_in_headline` is confirmed. Stored on new cases; older cases are read from their stored text.
- Event verification (`lib/sentinel/event-verify.ts` pure, `event-verify-v1`; runner `event-verify-run.ts`; `GET/POST /api/event-verification`; `components/sentinel/event-verification.tsx` in section A of the case sheet, 2026-11-13): on demand only, never in the hourly run. Separate from the company match. Reads at most 3 of the company's own 8-Ks filed 2 days before to 6 days after publication (plus up to 2 later 8-Ks with item 1.02/2.01, timeline only), each primary document and up to 2 EX-99 exhibits, via the SEC limiter, archived in R2/raw_event. States: not_checked (headline only) · supported (a filing names the headline's counterparty; says nothing of terms or effect) · insufficient · conflicting (a termination filed on or before publication vs an agreed/completed headline) · unavailable (incl. foreign issuers) · wrong_company and not_company_event (never examined). Stage comes from 8-K item numbers only. An amount alone never connects; a filing merely near the date confirms nothing; a missing filing is not falsity. Appends `event_verification` records; never changes a case, screen, score, ranking, outcome or Practice record.
- Market comparison (`lib/sentinel/market-model.ts` pure, `market-model-v1`; `components/sentinel/market-model.tsx` in section D of the case sheet, 2026-11-14): only with the on-request price context, from the same archived IEX bars (the case fetch now starts 400 days before publication). OLS with intercept of the stock's daily returns on SPY's, up to 252 matched days ending at the last close before publication (≥120 required, ≥90% coverage, stock's last pre-event bar within 3 sessions of SPY's); plain-English beta and fit ("most" only when R² > 0.5). After the event, 1/5/20 matched sessions: compounded stock, SPY, expected = Π(1+β·r_SPY)−1 (intercept NOT used), market-adjusted = stock − expected; the with-intercept variant, CAR and the simple stock − SPY are shown separately in details. Gross; prices split- and dividend-adjusted. Sessions closing after the assessment are labelled later. Stores nothing, changes no case, outcome, screen, score, ranking or Practice record. Peer comparison is unavailable (no point-in-time peer list); the pairs study brief is kept in `docs/PAIRS-STUDY-BRIEF.md`.
- Company page (`/company?symbol=`, `components/sentinel/company-page.tsx`; data `GET /api/company-page`, pure builder `lib/sentinel/company-page.ts` `company-page-v1`, 2026-11-14): one view per company from existing records. Owner's cases for the ticker (latest first; each event keeps its own match and verification), the latest case's existing rule-based view, evidence score and thesis summary (no new rule or label; stored conclusions shown as "Meets research rules" / "Watch: a rule is not met" / "Waiting for data" / "Older screen: no judgment"), 1M/6M/1Y IEX daily closes with SPY on the same dates and event markers at the first close after publication, key figures from reported annual SEC facts (change only vs the year before on a positive base; free cash flow = CFO − capex labelled as calculated; P/E only from the latest case), annual and quarterly revenue/net income with gaps shown, and virtual positions. Disabled directional-view slot. Reached from the case sheet and the idea cards. Read-only apart from the shared price archive. Design reference: the Lovable prototype "Signal Scout Investment Desk" (sample data, not used in code).
- Investment case (research prototype, `lib/sentinel/investment-case.ts` pure `investment-case-v1`; runner `investment-case-run.ts`; `GET/POST /api/investment-case`; `components/sentinel/investment-case.tsx` on the company page, 2026-11-15): implements spec v3.2 (Project doc `claude/sentinel-investment-case-spec-v3.2-2026-11-15.md`). On demand only. Checks with five states (supported / concern / unavailable / not checked / not applicable); unavailable is never 'not a concern'. Mechanisms: held up while the price fell, growth with valuation context, distress with a specific improvement, verified deal (never a priority alone); state precedence not applicable > contradicted > supported > not established. Verdict, first match wins: insufficient evidence → turnaround candidate → research priority → material concerns → watch. Every result says 'price opportunity: not established'. Data as known at the case's assessment time; one append-only `investment_case` record per case and version. **Not used for notifications, rankings, the screen, the score or any experiment.**
- Case overview (Site work by Codex, 2026-11-14, reconciled into GitHub 2026-11-15): `components/sentinel/case-overview.tsx` is the top of the case sheet (Overview / Financials / News tabs, plain-English sections), with the full thesis, idea card and figures in folds below. `lib/sentinel/plain-brief.ts` builds the readable summary from recorded facts; `lib/sentinel/investment-readiness.ts` (`investment-readiness-v1`) always returns `insufficient_for_recommendation` with its blockers. It is a decision-preparation note, not a verdict, and is separate from the investment-case prototype. Dark visual refresh across Brief, Discover, Following and Practice (`app/globals.css`).
- Price basis (`lib/sentinel/last-close.ts`, `price-basis-v2`, 2026-11-13): the P/E uses an eligible live IEX midpoint, or, when none exists (off-hours quotes are stale and very wide), the last completed IEX daily close, at most 5 days old, archived and labelled (`price.basis`). The screen rules did not change. Never compare outcomes across bases without saying so.
- Investment thesis panel (`lib/sentinel/thesis.ts`, `investment-thesis-v1`, `components/sentinel/thesis-panel.tsx`): first block of the case sheet. A why it appeared · B what supports a thesis · C what challenges it (supported points, then explicit unknowns) · D price and valuation · E research questions. Built from `whatChanged`, the event match and the on-request price context; each observation states its periods, filing and what it does not establish. A likely wrong-company case gets no supporting observations. The idea-list cards take their reason and counterpoint from the same object (`thesis.summary`).
- Idea card (`lib/sentinel/idea-card.ts`, `idea-card-v1`): the top of the case sheet. Sentinel's rule-based view, uncertainty, why now, for, against, unknowns and fit with virtual Practice holdings. Deterministic; no model text, no upside or probability, no buy/sell wording.
- Following monitor (`lib/sentinel/thesis-monitor.ts`, `GET /api/following`): per followed company, Broken / Weakening / Holding / Can't check / No thesis, from the owner's kill criteria only, plus what changed in the last 7 days (filings, news, criterion alerts, Sentinel cases). Unknown is never Holding.
- Navigation (2026-11-12): main tabs Brief (home) / Discover / Following / Practice; the rest under a Library menu. Old tab names map via `TAB_ALIAS` in `app/page.tsx`.
- Evidence score 0–100 (`evidence-score-v1`, see invariant 6): stored on every new discovery case with the quarterly revenue comparison it used; older cases are recomputed from their stored fields with the quarter unknown. The brief ranks strong cases and watch items by it.
- Case sheet "What changed?" (`lib/sentinel/case-context.ts` `case-context-v1`, `GET /api/case-context`, `components/sentinel/what-changed.tsx`): event times, SEC changes from the evidence pack with valid comparisons only, rule readings (the Research team's template), P/E with limitations, expectations always `unknown` (no data). Price context (`price-context-v1`) only on request (`?prices=1`): Alpaca IEX daily bars archived in `raw_event` and reused for 6 h; 20 sessions before the last close before publication (known at case time) and up to 20 after (flagged as later information). It is not sent to any model and not part of the hourly run. User-facing label for v2 INVESTIGATE: "Meets research rules" (stored value unchanged).
- Morning brief (`/brief`, `GET /api/morning-brief`, builder `lib/sentinel/morning-brief.ts`): executive summary of the last 24 h, bottom line first. Every sentence is assembled from stored records and rule templates (no model text, no estimated numbers); only `research-screen-v2` cases become takes or radar; since 2026-11-15 only good-news cases are 'worth a closer look', neutral rule-meeting cases count with the watch list, and bad or mixed news is only counted.

### How it runs and deploys (important)

- **GitHub `main` is the single source of truth.** Every change reaches the Site from GitHub `main`. Never edit the Site's source directly. On 2026-11-15 the Site had 20 files of work that GitHub did not have; publishing `main` would have deleted them. They were reconciled through the branch `site-snapshot-2026-11-15`. Before any publish, confirm that the Site's tree equals the commit being published plus nothing else.

- **Hosting is ChatGPT Sites.** Deployment happens from the Sites project, not from GitHub. A merged PR is not live until it is deployed there.
- **There is no scheduler in this code.** The hourly run is an external ChatGPT scheduled task calling the MCP tool `refresh_saved_company_filings` (`app/mcp/route.ts`), which runs filings, news, discovery, assessment and outcomes in one request.
- **Auth trusts platform headers** (`oai-authenticated-user-id`, `oai-authenticated-user-email`) and `getChatGPTUser()`. These are safe only because the Sites platform sets them. **Before hosting anywhere else, replace authentication first**, or anyone can impersonate the owner.

## Known issues (from the 2026-11-12 audit; fix deliberately, not in passing)

1. Fixed by research-screen-v2 on 2026-11-12 for new cases; historical `INVESTIGATE` from `research-screen-v1` is a data-completeness gate (company named + P/E computable + recent revenue). The P/E value, valuation, price move and news direction do not affect it. INVESTIGATE-vs-WAIT outcomes are therefore confounded with data quality.
2. Fixed by research-screen-v2 on 2026-11-12 for new records; previously `INVESTIGATE` was hard-coded on every raw discovery candidate (`discovery.ts`) and every watchlist news case (`app/mcp/route.ts`), giving one label three meanings.
3. **Fixed 2026-11-12:** outcomes are recorded at 5, 20, 60 and 120 matched trading-day intervals, one record per horizon (`horizonIntervals`). Pre-existing records are the 5-interval horizon. Never compare across horizons.
4. **Improved 2026-11-12 (twice):** `observeOutcomes` checks up to 8 (case, horizon) pairs per run within 20 s. Due pairs come from three light reads in one D1 batch (`dueOutcomePairs`: case ids with only symbol/cik, outcome keys, cooling-down attempts), not one correlated query per pair. Bars are fetched once per symbol per run and SPY once per run, so each raw payload is written once, not once per horizon. At most 400 of the oldest complete cases are scanned per run. Not yet verified against production D1.
5. Capacity: ~250 headlines collected vs ≤6 assessed per run; the whole pipeline in one request is the likely cause of partial scans.
6. From September: **fixed 2026-11-12** — replay now recomputes and compares (mismatch raises an operational alert); `evaluateEntity` passes an explicit memo; alerts disclose estimated values. **By design, not a bug:** effective tax rate (and so NOPAT/ROIC) is unavailable for loss years — unavailable beats wrong. **Decided 2026-11-12 (founder: keep both):** `consecutive_periods` counts fiscal years by default; a comparison may opt in to `"period":"quarter"` (revenue, net_income, operating_income, cfo, capex, eps_diluted only; anything else is unknown) and `"basis":"yoy_pct"` (vs the same quarter a year earlier). The unreported fiscal Q4 does not break a quarterly streak; any other gap is unknown. Following (`thesis-monitor-v2`): quarterly-only trips show as Weakening, annual or event trips as Broken.

7. **Found and fixed 2026-11-13 for new cases:** off-hours, the IEX latest quote is stale and very wide, so no case assessed outside US market hours could get a P/E; all 75 screen-v2 cases on the live Site were WAIT. See price basis above. Stored cases are not rewritten. Manual Practice still shows an unknown value off-hours for the same reason.
8. **Measured 2026-11-13 (`scripts/db-workload.mjs`):** the load came from the assessment queue pick (two correlated subqueries, six times a run: 11 million rows per call at a week of data), not from the missing index. Fixed by `discoveryQueue`. The index `signal_log(owner, kind, created_at)` made that old query 35× slower in the test, so it was not shipped; it still helps small unbounded reads and needs testing against every query before a migration. Measure before changing a query on `signal_log`.

## Automated Practice experiment (virtual only — added 2026-11-13)

`lib/sentinel/auto-practice.ts` (pure rules, `auto-practice-v1`), `lib/sentinel/auto-practice-run.ts`, `app/api/auto-practice/route.ts`, `components/sentinel/auto-practice.tsx`.

- The founder asked for it explicitly. It is the prospective validation invariant 7 requires before any buy/sell label; it is not such a label, not an alert and not advice.
- **Off until the owner starts it** (`auto_practice_control`). Then one bounded step at the end of the hourly run, plus a manual check. Stopping means no new positions; open ones still close under the exit rule. A new start is a new experiment with its own ledger.
- Rules (`AUTO_POLICY`, `AUTO_RULES_TEXT`): only cases assessed after the start; screen v2 INVESTIGATE + `security-identity-v2` ordinary share + `named_in_headline`; $1,000 per position, max 5, one per company, max 2 opens per check, oldest assessment first; fresh narrow IEX quotes for the stock and SPY (so market hours only); 96-hour entry window; 30-day time exit; 15 bp simulated cost per side; SPY pays the same costs.
- **Not filtered by news direction (2026-11-15):** `auto-practice-v1` still opens any eligible screen-v2 INVESTIGATE case, good, neutral or bad news, because its rules are fixed. Excluding bad news needs a new version and a new experiment (founder decision).
- **Changing a rule means a new version and a new experiment.** Never edit `AUTO_POLICY` for a running one.
- Records are append-only: one `auto_practice_decision` per considered case (an open or a final skip with its reason), `auto_practice_close`, and one `auto_practice_run` per check (so the last check is always known). Writes are conditional on the ledger row count, and ids derive from the case, so overlapping or retried runs cannot double-spend or double-open.
- Separate from manual Practice (`practice_*` records are never read or written by it), from the screen, score, rankings and alerts. No broker, no orders, no model, no user conviction invented.
- It proves nothing until enough trades close (`MIN_CLOSED_FOR_RECORD` = 20) and it is compared with the outcomes of all assessed cases.

## Decision workflow (on demand — added 2026-11-16)

Founder decision: four separate questions per case, never one threshold or one combined score. (1) Business quality: investment case and evidence score, unchanged. (2) Market expectations: what the price assumes. (3) Investment opportunity: shown beside (2), always "not established" today. (4) Portfolio suitability: what acting would change. **Banned wording in this workflow:** undervalued, overvalued, cheap, expensive, upside, target, buy, sell, should (tests enforce it on every output).

- **Share count (3A):** `parseShareCounts` in `financial.ts` reads `dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding` as metric `shares_outstanding` (instant fact: `period_end` = cover-page date, `known_from` = filing date). Several values in one filing or on one date are never summed: one marker fact `shares_outstanding_multiclass` instead. Not in `TAGS`, so `derive` and `investment-case-v1` are unchanged. Existing companies get the facts at their next ingest.
- **What the price assumes (3B):** `lib/sentinel/implied-expectations.ts` (pure, `implied-expectations-v1`), `GET /api/implied-expectations?symbol=`, `components/sentinel/implied-expectations.tsx` on the company page. Equity basis only: price × cover-page shares against reported CFO − capex (same validity checks and `DCF_ASSUMPTIONS` as `cashflow-scenarios.ts`: 12%, 2%, 5 years). Bisection for the constant 5-year growth within −30%…+40%, else "outside range". 3×3 sensitivity. Reported history beside it. Net debt is context only. Unavailable with the exact reason for: financial company (SIC 6000–6799), base cash flow ≤ 0 or unmatched or older than 550 days, missing or multi-class share count, price missing or older than 5 days, debt above 4× operating cash flow. Untagged debt, noncontrolling interests and preferred stock are "not checked", never clear. Price = last completed IEX daily close.
- **Three-way comparison (3C):** `lib/sentinel/decision-compare.ts` (pure, `decision-compare-v1`), `POST /api/decision-compare`. Keep / add the stock / add SPY from the same Practice state (holdings valued at the last completed IEX close) and budget (default $1,000): cash, 15 bp entry and exit costs, stock weight, and the portfolio under SPY −10% / 0 / +10% through each holding's measured beta (≥120 matched days, else unavailable; never assumed). Optional `practice_mandate` record is read if present; none exists yet ("no mandate set"). ES95 not computed.
- **Snapshot and review (3D):** `lib/sentinel/decision-snapshot.ts` (pure), runner `decision-run.ts`, `GET/POST /api/decision-snapshot`, `components/sentinel/decision-compare.tsx`. Append-only `decision_snapshot` (id from owner + case + request id; stored once) with price, implied expectations, Practice state, the three options, the SPY reference and one checkpoint from fixed templates. Reopening returns the stored text unchanged plus a review computed now. Reviews are separate `decision_review` records (one per UTC day) with three results that are never merged: business expectation, investment performance net of costs, and against SPY and keeping the portfolio. `GET ?view=replay` replays stored cases (up to 10 symbols per request).
- **Funnel counts (3F):** `lib/sentinel/discovery-funnel.ts` (pure, `discovery-funnel-v1`), `GET /api/discovery-funnel?hours=24|96|168`, a fold on Discover that loads only when opened. Read-only; the collector, queue and batch are untouched. Unit = (report, symbol) pair, definitions as `discoveryQueue()`. "Deduplicated" is not recorded. Reconciliation checks are returned and any difference is displayed.
- **Never** part of the hourly run; never reads or writes the screen, score, rankings, alerts, Practice trades or either experiment.
- **Gap found on real filings (2026-11-16), addressed by `xbrl-tags-v2` below for the decision workflow only:** `TAGS.capex` (v1) holds only `PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment`; EMCOR reports `PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets`. v1 readers are unchanged.
- **Review fixes, batch 1 (2026-11-16):**

  - *implied-expectations-v2:* ADR rule (share count from a 20-F/40-F → unavailable: ADR ratio not stored); a visible "Checks not run" line (`notRun`); SEC load state in the result. Stored v1 snapshots keep their v1 output.
  - *Snapshots:* refused (HTTP 422) when no price is available; the browser sends one request id per comparison and ignores a second click while saving (`lib/sentinel/save-guard.ts`).
  - *Checkpoints:* `overdue` after `dueBy` with no qualifying filing, and every not-yet/overdue result says which filing it waits for. Overdue is not a "no".
  - *Comparison:* up to 20 companies priced per comparison; holdings over it are "not priced: over the limit of 20 companies per comparison", never valued.
- **Valuation safety (2026-11-17, batch 2, `implied-expectations-v3`, `xbrl-tags-v3`, `facts-parser-v3`):**
  - *Stock splits:* `splitCheck` compares unadjusted and split-only IEX closes (`archivedDailyBars(..., 'raw'|'split')`; the `'all'` archive key is unchanged) on the cover-page session and the price session. A split since the share count → "Unavailable: split since share count"; a factor that is not a usual ratio → "Unavailable: possible split since share count"; a check that cannot run → "Unavailable: split check not run (…)". The share count is never adjusted and pre- and post-split numbers are never mixed.
  - *Amended filings:* several share classes = more than one distinct value on the same cover date inside one filing. A 10-K/A or 10-Q/A is its own filing: a repeated or corrected count is a revision (the most recently filed wins), never a second class.
  - *Share classes:* summed only if every class count is present. companyfacts never labels a count with its class, so no stored source can show that; another ticker under the same SEC company (SEC `BRK-B` = IEX `BRK.B`) or several counts in one filing → "Unavailable: share classes", and "Checks not run: share classes".
  - *Old counts:* a cover-page count more than 400 days before the price → no market value.
  - *Noncontrolling interests and preferred stock* (`xbrl-tags-v3` = v2 + `nci` `MinorityInterest`, `nci_redeemable` `RedeemableNoncontrollingInterestEquityCarryingAmount`, `preferred` `PreferredStockValue` / `PreferredStockValueOutstanding`; v1 and v2 unchanged), at the base cash-flow year-end: above 10% of market value (book value) → unavailable with the reason; tag present but unreadable (a unit other than USD: `claim_unreadable` marker fact) or reported only for other dates within 400 days → unavailable with the reason; nothing reported → "not reported in the company's filings" (a note, not a check not run); company read before `facts-parser-v3` → "Checks not run".
  - *Wording:* banned words removed from existing user-facing text, including negated uses (idea-select, idea-card, investment-readiness, case overview, discovery, investment brief, plain brief, price assessment, thesis, discovery case, MCP tool text, the old ledger's labels "Practice purchase/sale"). Stored values `buy`/`sell` in `paper_trade` records are unchanged. Two tests guard it: every string in `lib/`, `components/` and `app/` (TypeScript AST, small explained allow-list) and every string the routes return across all test owners and cases.
- Offline research only: `scripts/research/` (`implied-offline.mjs`, `harness.mjs`). Specs: `docs/specs/prediction-feasibility-2026-11-16.md`, `docs/specs/architecture-handoff-2026-11-16.md`.

## Research evaluation (measurement only — added 2026-11-25)

Spec and audit: `docs/specs/research-evaluation-stage1-2026-11-25.md`.

- **Shared statistics** (`lib/sentinel/research-stats.ts`, `research-stats-v1`, pure): costs 15 bp per side on the stock **and** SPY (matching SPY = 0 excess); a valid return is finite and above −100%; median of an even count = average of the two middle values; seeded circular moving-block bootstrap over decision dates, block length from window overlap. Use it for any new return measurement; do not write another cost or median.
- **Harness** (`scripts/research/harness.mjs`, `harness-v2`, offline): rows carry `exitDate`; earlier-set rows whose window reaches the next set are purged (plus an optional embargo) and counted; invalid rows are rejected with a reason.
- **Selection comparison** (`lib/sentinel/selection-comparison.ts` pure `selection-comparison-v1`; `GET /api/selection-comparison?horizon=5|20|60|120`, default 20; no page uses it): screen-v2 matched cases, selected = `meetsIdeaRules` (exported from `idea-select.ts`, the list's own rule) vs every other compared case, SPY on the same matched dates, from stored `discovery_outcome` records only. Entry must be after the case's UTC date; misaligned or invalid records are rejected with reasons; missing = not yet due vs due with no record; one horizon per comparison; a company's overlapping later case in the same group is left out. Each case records its news-direction version and whether the label was stored or computed; mixed versions are never pooled. **Below 20 usable outcomes in either group: counts only.** Read-only, on request, never in the hourly run; never feeds the list, screen, score, rankings, alerts or experiments.
- **Known defect, not fixed (founder decides):** the decision replay (`replayDecisions` in `decision-run.ts`) enters at the last close at or before the case time, so it credits moves that happened before anyone could act; exits are "latest". Do not use replay figures for evaluation until `decision-replay-v2` (entry at the first close after the case, fixed horizons) is approved.

## Shadow reader (evaluation only — added 2026-11-12)

`lib/sentinel/shadow-reader.ts`, `lib/sentinel/shadow-store.ts`, `app/api/shadow-comparison/route.ts`.

- NVIDIA Nemotron (free developer API) reads discovery headlines **in parallel with the rules** and its output is stored as `shadow_reading` records. It **must not feed** any conclusion, rule, queue order, label or alert until the founder approves it, based on the labelled comparison.
- Off unless the Site variable `SHADOW_READER_ENABLED` is exactly `"1"`. Key: Site secret `NVIDIA_API_KEY`. Model: optional `SHADOW_MODEL` (defaults to Nemotron 3 Nano Omni).
- Runs **after** the batch has created its cases (max 10 readings, 20 s), so it cannot change what was assessed. Every failure is recorded as `unavailable`/`invalid`, never thrown.
- Sends only the public headline, provider summary, SEC company name and ticker. Nothing from journal, memos, watchlist or positions.
- Code, not the model, enforces: strict schema, verbatim quotes, no numbers outside quotes (invariant 5). Stored rows are built from an allow-list; the key cannot be stored.
- Comparison: `GET /api/shadow-comparison`, labelling sheet at `?format=csv`.

## Research team (on demand, per case — added 2026-11-12)

`lib/sentinel/evidence-pack.ts`, `lib/sentinel/research-team.ts`, `app/api/research-team/route.ts`, `components/sentinel/research-team-panel.tsx` (in the case sheet).

- **Evidence pack (`evidence-pack-v1`):** deterministic, built from the stored case plus SEC figures filed **and** recorded by the case's assessment time. Every item has an id, source (URL or null), source and capture times, period, unit, currency, confidence and staleness. Missing data is listed, not filled. Only `shareable` items (public headline, provider summary, SEC identity and SEC facts) may reach a model. Quotes, the screen, the score and anything private are never sent.
- **Run:** synchronous, at most one model call (20 s cap, no retries), only when the user clicks. It is never part of the hourly run. It is off unless the Site variable `RESEARCH_TEAM_ENABLED` is exactly `"1"`. It reuses `NVIDIA_API_KEY`, uses the fixed NVIDIA endpoint, and accepts only models on the allow-list (`RESEARCH_MODEL` optional). When the model is off or fails, a deterministic **rules template** fills the panel, labelled "no AI model ran".
- **Code enforces the output contract:** a strict schema, citations limited to this pack's shareable ids, verbatim quotes, numbers that must appear in the cited sources, and no buy/sell/hold, targets or probabilities. Failing statements are dropped and logged, never repaired. A credential-like answer is discarded unread.
- **Records:** append-only `research_run` rows (id = the client's request id, so a retry returns the stored run). A 20 s cooldown applies per case. The research team never changes a case, screen, score, ranking, outcome, alert or trade.
- Offline only: `lib/sentinel/event-backtest.ts`. Next steps: `docs/RESEARCH-ROADMAP.md`.
